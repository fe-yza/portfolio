01 — What BCO is

A room where Ottawa's builders ship together.

Builders Collective Ottawa is the city's cross-disciplinary tech community — 500+ people who'd rather build something this weekend than talk about it next quarter. We run biweekly Co-Hacks, bring global tech companies to Ottawa, and connect our members with local founders, investors and the press.

04 — The Co-Hack format

Show up. Ship something.

Not a lecture, not a mixer. A Co-Hack is a working session built for momentum — the format at the core of a 500-person community.

  • Pitch what you're buildingEveryone shares a project, a problem or an idea in under a minute.
  • Find your peopleEngineers meet designers, founders meet marketers. Teams form on the spot.
  • Heads-down hackingReal building time, with the room around you to unblock you.
  • Demo & feedbackShow progress, get honest feedback, come back in two weeks with more.
